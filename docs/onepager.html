Sahayak · सहायक · Ideas for India 2026

An offline scam shield and benefits guide for people new to digital money, in Hindi and English, by voice or touch.
"Is this a scam?" Paste, speak, photograph or describe a message, a call or a UPI QR code. In milliseconds: Scam, Suspicious, No scam signs (never "safe"), or Could not check (a language it cannot read yet: safe-default advice, no false green). Then the reasons, what to do (money lost: call 1930; an attempt: report on Chakshu) and a complaint draft. A QR card says: scanning sends money, never brings it in.
"What am I owed?" A short spoken interview finds which of 12 central schemes a person can get, health first: Ayushman Bharat pays up to ₹5 lakh a year for hospital admission (not OPD); at 70+, the Vay Vandana card (cover shared with a 70+ spouse). Then pensions, PMSBY, PMJJBY, APY, PM-KISAN, e-Shram, PM-SYM, Jan Dhan: the papers, the office, what to say, and a printed slip with a QR for the CSC operator.

How it works

  • One node at a CSC or bank-agent counter (a laptop or mini-PC). Phones join its Wi-Fi, which has no internet; the app is a cached web page, no install.
  • At home, too: a phone that has opened Sahayak's secure web address once (the public site today; the node's own after a one-time HTTPS setup) keeps its own scam check and benefits interview, with no node or internet.
  • Named signals decide, not a model: 64 signals in a signed content pack (lookalike links, .bank.in rule, UPI "scan to receive", OTP asks, digital arrest, "pay for your Ayushman card"…), a pattern matcher and a small classifier. Vetted templates explain each verdict; an optional local LLM (off by default) may add a line only through a safety gate.
  • Scheme rules as data, each traced to its official page and date; yes / likely / unknown / no; asks only what can change a result.
  • Offline voice both ways on the node; amounts spoken as words; "I heard …, is that right?" before any answer is used.
  • Operator console: "Ask the agent" queue, assisted checks read aloud, case log, 58 mm slips, counters with no personal data.

Privacy and safety

  • Messages, voices and answers stay in the node's memory for at most 15 minutes; nothing typed or said is written to disk.
  • Case log only with consent: no names or numbers, encrypted, deleted after 30 days, one "delete everything" button.
  • Green means "no signs found, still verify", never "safe"; a language it cannot read gets a grey "could not check". Every amount shown comes from a signed pack. Content updates are Ed25519-signed; an altered pack is refused.
