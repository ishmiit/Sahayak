# Letters of intent: templates

Use these only after a partner has seen Sahayak working (ideally after the field morning), and only with words they
agree with. Each is non-binding: it records interest in a pilot, not a contract, money or data sharing. Print on the
partner's letterhead if they have one; a signed plain page with a stamp is fine for a CSC. Fill the brackets, delete
what does not apply, and never overstate what Sahayak has done.

---

## 1. CSC village-level entrepreneur (VLE) or bank business correspondent

**Subject: Letter of intent to host a pilot of Sahayak at [CSC name / BC point], [village, block, district]**

I, [name], operate [CSC ID / BC agent code] at [address]. On [date] the Sahayak team (Roshan Raj, Ishmiit Singh)
showed me Sahayak, an offline scam check and benefits guide that runs on one computer at my counter and on people's
phones, in Hindi and English, without sending anything to the internet. [On [date] we tried it with [number] people
who came to my centre.]

I intend to host a pilot of Sahayak at my centre for [three / six] months, starting [month], subject to the
equipment being provided and to agreement on the details. During the pilot I will:

- keep the Sahayak node switched on during working hours and help visitors use it;
- use the operator console for walk-in checks and benefits slips;
- share the monthly impact counters (counts only: no names, numbers or messages) with the team and [district
  official / bank], and give feedback.

I understand that Sahayak gives guidance only, that the office decides on every scheme, and that I must not
charge anyone for using Sahayak.

Signature, name, CSC ID / agent code, phone, date, stamp

**हिंदी (सार):** मैं, [नाम], [CSC/BC पॉइंट] पर [तीन/छह] महीने के लिए सहायक का पायलट चलाने का इरादा रखता/रखती हूँ:
नोड चालू रखना, लोगों की मदद करना, हर महीने की गिनती (बिना नाम या नंबर) साझा करना। सहायक के इस्तेमाल के लिए किसी से
पैसे नहीं लूँगा/लूँगी। यह पत्र कोई अनुबंध नहीं है।

## 2. NGO or self-help-group federation

**Subject: Letter of intent to support a field pilot of Sahayak in [block, district]**

[Organisation] works with [who: women's SHGs, older people, migrant workers] in [area] on [what]. We have seen Sahayak
[and took part in its field test on [date]].

We intend to support a pilot of Sahayak in [number] villages of [block] from [month] for [duration], subject to
agreement on the details, by:

- introducing the team to [CSCs / SHG meetings] and helping invite participants;
- reviewing the Hindi [and [language]] wording and the consent process with our field staff;
- helping collect, with each person's consent and with identifying details removed, real suspicious messages so the
  team can test Sahayak on them.

This letter states intent only. It creates no financial or legal obligation. [Organisation] will not receive or hold
personal data from Sahayak.

Signature, name, designation, organisation, registration number, date

## 3. Bank branch, regional office or State-Level Bankers' Committee (SLBC) convenor

**Subject: Expression of interest in a pilot of Sahayak at business-correspondent points in [district]**

We have reviewed Sahayak, an offline scam check and benefits guide for first-time digital-payment users, which runs
at the counter and on the customer's phone, keeps no personal data, and points every scam verdict to the 1930
helpline. We see value in helping customers recognise fraud before they pay or share a code, and in the monthly
counts of scam types seen at our BC points.

We are interested in a pilot at [number] BC points in [district] for [duration], subject to our internal approvals,
information-security review and an agreement on scope. This expression of interest is not a commitment to fund or
procure.

Signature, name, designation, branch / office, date
