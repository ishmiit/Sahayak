Try Sahayak yourself

Sahayak runs on the laptop at our stand. Its Wi-Fi has no internet. Nothing you type, say or photograph leaves it.
  1. Join the Wi-Fi named on the node's sticker, then scan the sticker's QR code (or open any web address): the app opens. No install.
  2. Check a message. Tap "Is this a scam?", then type a card's message, say it with the mic, or choose Screenshot and photograph the card.
  3. Check a UPI QR. Choose QR code and photograph a Q card. Sahayak shows who the money would go to and how much.
  4. Find benefits. Tap "What am I owed?" and answer as one of the P cards. Print the slip if a printer is attached.
  5. Try to fool it. Write your own message, in Hindi, English or Hinglish. Tell us what it got wrong; we log every miss.
  6. Watch the node page on the laptop: the count of connections Sahayak made to the internet stays at 0.
What to look for. A verdict in milliseconds with the reasons in Hindi and English; "No signs found" never says "safe"; every amount comes from a signed pack; the app always asks "I heard …, is that right?" before using a spoken answer.
About the QR cards: they use the UPI handle @sahayakdemo, which does not exist. A real UPI app will refuse them, so they can never pay anyone. Scan them only with Sahayak.

The answer key is on the last page. It was produced by running Sahayak itself on every card.
